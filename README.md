# Foquita Gyu Restaurant

Foquita Gyu Restaurant is a text adventure game made in Python. A very demanding customer has been waiting half an hour for his food, the chef turned into a seal, and now everything depends on you.

![Game](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a0f333-599a-7db8-bee0-dd040d3b9845/image.png)

## What does it do?
You move around the restaurant by typing numbers: you can go to the entrance, the dining room, or the kitchen. Inside the kitchen there are two challenges to get ingredients: a riddle in the pantry and a word scramble in the fridge. If you collect 2 ingredients and bring them to the customer, you win. If you show up empty-handed, the customer complains and sends you back to the entrance.

I hope you like it. Thank you for playing, and don't worry about the chef: as a seal, he is very happy.
